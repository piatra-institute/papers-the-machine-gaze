# Brief

## Question

The origin conversation asks whether machines are machines, starts from the self-sorting arrays of Zhang, Goldstein and Levin (sorting algorithms that show regularities their code does not specify), and develops a "machine gaze": a way of seeing that reduces people and living systems to the variables that make them usable, which capitalism, Taylorism and data systems turn into practice. It proposes machinehood as a task-relative quotient, a performative extension of Rosen's modeling relation, an engineering reading (observability, controllability, model reduction, information bottleneck), "machinic capture", and three computational demonstrations, and it lists a constellation of thinkers (Rosen, Wimsatt, Marx, Mumford, Foucault, Heidegger, Deleuze, Scott, Hacking, MacKenzie, Canguilhem, Simondon, Ashby, Beer, Whitehead). The question for the paper: what is a precise, checkable definition of being a machine under a description, what follows from it, and what does a description leave out when it is used to act on a system?

## Claim

1. A system is a machine under an observation when its dynamics close on the observed variables (fibre condition; lumpability; commutation of Rosen's modeling relation; Kalman's result that input-output relations determine only the controllable and observable part). Machinehood is relative to the observation and not arbitrary.
2. Self-sorting arrays reproduced: all compositions compute the same function; mixed arrays cluster by algorithm during sorting; at the same observed sortedness, the remaining work differs by about an order of magnitude between algorithms, so the progress observable is not closed.
3. A controller designed on a reduced modal model is stable on its model and destabilizes the plant through combined control and observation spillover unless the unmodelled modes are damped; either spillover alone is harmless.
4. Machinization: in an organisation whose throughput is observed and whose disturbance-absorbing repertoire is not, ascent on observed throughput drives maintenance to zero, at which point the system is exactly closed under its observation and long-run output is far below the optimum.
5. In the loop between model and system, the share of disagreement removed by changing the system is kappa/(kappa+eta).
