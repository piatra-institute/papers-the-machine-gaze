# Editorial record

Status: full read and revision pending.

## Contribution and reader

What question can this paper answer, for whom, and at what level of confidence?

## Material worth preserving

Identify particular passages, examples, results or distinctions.

## Reverse outline

For each section: what does it add to the reasoning? Note gaps and repetitions.

## Strongest rival

State a serious alternative account, with sources and consequences for the thesis.

## Decisions and remaining checks

Record argument, structure and voice changes. Separate verified source support,
executed tests and visual inspection from work still needed. Do not stamp a
completed review until the final manuscript has been read.
